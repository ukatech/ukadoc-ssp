# Calendar: Menu

This menu appears when you **right-click** the calendar.<br>
The functions of each menu item are as follows.

## Edit schedule

This edits the schedule, just like when you **double-click** the calendar.

## Read schedule

The ghost reads the schedule for that day aloud.

## Today's schedule

Reads aloud today's schedule, regardless of the selected date.

## Delete all

Deletes registered schedules.<br>
You can choose to delete schedules in bulk by the following units.<br>
Be careful: all schedules in the selected unit will be deleted without confirmation.

- <strong>『This day』</strong> - Deletes the schedule for the selected day.
- <strong>『This month』</strong> - Deletes the schedule for the selected month.
- <strong>『This year』</strong> - Deletes the schedule for the selected year.
- <strong>『Everything before this day』</strong> - Deletes all schedules before the selected day.

## Previous month

Displays the previous month.

## Next month

Displays the next month.

## Display

You can choose to display the calendar in front of other windows or always in the center of the screen.

- <strong>『Always on top』</strong> - Keeps the calendar visible so it is not hidden behind normal windows.
- <strong>『Always show in the center of the screen』</strong> - Always displays the window in the center of the screen when it opens.

## Skin

Select the calendar's appearance.<br>
If multiple skins are registered, more options will be available.<br>
This works just like selecting a ghost or shell.

## Schedule sensor

Gets schedules from a server.<br>
If multiple schedule sensors are registered, more options will be available here too.<br>
Retrieved schedules are registered automatically.<br>
Only schedules for the displayed month are retrieved, and duplicate items are not registered.

## Close

Closes the calendar.
