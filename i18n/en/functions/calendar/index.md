# Calendar

Like typical schedule management software, the Calendar lets you manage various appointments and anniversaries.<br>
SSP has the following distinctive features.

## Ghost reads out your schedule

The ghost reads out your schedule when you right-click a date and select “Today’s schedule,” or when it is five minutes before a scheduled event with a specified time.

That alone would be no different from an ordinary scheduler, but ghosts that support calendar events can respond in various ways depending on the schedule.<br>
For example, if the schedule type is “Deadline,” the ghost might look flustered.

You can also have the ghost read out past or upcoming schedules by hovering over the mark below the date and leaving the cursor there.

## Share schedules over the network

The Schedule sensor lets you retrieve schedules from the internet and post schedules online.<br>
The available features differ depending on the Schedule sensor.<br>
For details, see the documentation for the Schedule sensor you installed.

(The “Register schedule” feature may not be implemented.)

## Calendar skins

You can change the Calendar’s appearance by switching skins.<br>
The layout may vary greatly between skin data, but the features remain the same.
