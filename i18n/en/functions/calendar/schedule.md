# Calendar: Edit schedule

**Double-click** a date in the Calendar to edit an existing schedule or add a new one.

## \[yyyy/mm/dd\] Schedule

The day’s schedules are listed here.<br>
Schedules are sorted by start time.<br>
**Click** an individual schedule to edit its details.

## Required fields

- **Title**<br>
  The text entered here becomes the schedule title.
- **Start**<br>
  Specifies the schedule’s start date and time.<br>
  Check “Enable start time” to specify a start time as well.
- **End**<br>
  Specifies the schedule’s end date and time.<br>
  Check “Enable end date and time” to specify an end date and time as well.

## Optional fields

- **Type**<br>
  Specifies the event’s general category.<br>
  Some ghosts respond differently depending on this value.

- **Notification sound**<br>
  As the specified time approaches, the content is displayed in a balloon and the sound specified here is played.<br>
  Click the “Browse...” button to select a file.

- **Details**<br>
  Additional information about the schedule. You can enter details over multiple lines. This field is optional.

- **Repeat**

  Lets you set schedules that occur weekly, monthly, or yearly.

  - **None**: A one-time schedule
  - **Weekly**: Repeats every week on the specified day
  - **Monthly**: Repeats every month on the specified day
  - **Yearly**: Repeats every year on the specified month and day

- **Sakura Script**<br>
  Enter detailed information about the schedule as Sakura Script for the ghost to speak. You do not need to enter anything if you do not want the ghost to say anything in particular. You can enter multiple lines.

## Dialog buttons

- **“New” button**<br>
  Click this to start entering a new schedule after viewing the registered schedules on the left.
- **“Delete” button**<br>
  Click this after viewing a registered schedule on the left to delete that schedule.
- **“Close” button**<br>
  Closes the schedule editing dialog. Your changes are saved automatically.

## Post schedule

Select a destination, then **click** the “Post” button to instruct the Schedule sensor to register the schedule.<br>
Depending on the Schedule sensor, nothing may happen.
