# Basic usage: Playing with ghosts

What a ghost does and what it can do depends entirely on that ghost.<br>
That said, you may not know what to do at first, so here we’ll introduce some features that many ghosts have in common.

## Talking

If you start a ghost and leave it running, most ghosts will talk on their own.<br>
Ghosts are generally made up of a pair—the “main character” and their “partner”—so it might be more accurate to say that they perform comedy routines.

!!! tip "ヒント"

    There are ghosts with one character or three or more characters, too. For example, the ghost “Emily/Phase4.5” features three characters who banter with one another.<br>
    ![Three characters in Emily/Phase4.5](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/0.png){ width="400" }

## Interacting with the cursor

You can enjoy watching a ghost, but it should also react in various ways when you use the cursor to pet, poke, pull, or hit it.<br>
Try different things and see how it reacts.

However, the places you can interact with and the reactions you get vary from ghost to ghost, and sometimes from shell to shell.<br>
There are also interactions we can’t cover here. They’re part of what gives each ghost its own unique personality.

!!! tip "ヒント"

    The cursor may change shape over places you can interact with.<br>
    ![Interactive areas and the cursor](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/1.png)

### Moving the cursor

**Move the cursor up, down, left, or right** over specific parts of a character to pet or touch them.<br>
For example, moving the cursor near the head lets you pet many ghosts.<br>
(Some ghosts are less responsive, but that’s part of their personality.)<br>
![Petting by moving the cursor](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/4.png)

### Dragging

You can move characters and balloons (speech bubbles) by **dragging** them (including the partner).<br>
Some characters may complain if you overlap them with their partner...<br>
![Moving a character by dragging](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/2.png)

While dragging, characters snap into place at the edge of the monitor or between characters (they automatically adjust their positions to line up neatly). Press the Ctrl key to disable snapping, if you don’t want it.

### Mouse wheel

**Turn the mouse wheel** to trigger actions such as pulling or flipping something.<br>
However, since this feature was introduced relatively recently, ghosts may interpret it in many different ways, such as using it to change how often they talk. The response may also vary depending on where you use it.<br>
![A playful gust of wind from the mouse wheel](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/3.png)

### Double-clicking

**Double-click** to trigger actions such as poking, hitting, or grabbing.<br>
They may even react as if they’re hurt, depending on where you click.<br>
![Don’t be rough](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/8.png)

Also, when you **double-click** a blank area (where the cursor doesn’t change), most ghosts display their own menu in the balloon.<br>
We’ll explain this menu next.

## Menu

![Ghost menu](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/5.png){ width="400" }<br>
For most ghosts, **double-clicking** a blank area opens a menu.<br>
You can use this menu to run or configure features unique to that ghost.

!!! tip "ヒント"

    Even when ghosts have the same feature, they often use different names for its menu item.

### Listen to the ghost

Ask the ghost to say something.<br>
In the ghost “Emily/Phase4.5,” this item is called “Say something.”

### Talk to the ghost

A box opens where you can enter a word to talk to the ghost.<br>
It may not understand anything too complicated, but it will probably respond to a greeting.<br>
The ghost “Emily/Phase4.5” doesn’t have this item.<br>
![Communicate box](../assets/screenshots/howto/use/6.png)

### Talking frequency

Set how often the ghost talks.<br>
In the ghost “Emily/Phase4.5,” this item appears when you select “Ask Emily.”

## Key input

After focusing a ghost (click it once to bring it to the foreground), you may get a response by pressing certain keys.<br>
These keys often work like shortcuts for specific features.

For example, in the ghost “Emily/Phase4.5,” pressing the t key makes it start talking right away.<br>
This may be easier than selecting “Say something” from the menu.

!!! tip "ヒント"

    Key input often won’t work unless your input mode is set to half-width characters.<br>
    If it doesn’t work, check that you haven’t switched to full-width mode.

## Ghost-specific interactions

To start with, try out the different interactions introduced above.

However, as mentioned at the beginning of this page, what a ghost can do varies from ghost to ghost.<br>
Some ghosts may not support the interactions described here, while many others can do much more.<br>
Check the ghost’s readme for information about its specific interactions.

!!! tip "ヒント"

    You can open the readme for the ghost you currently have running by opening the right-click menu, selecting the fifth item from the bottom (Information), and then clicking the second item from the top in the submenu.<br>
    Note that menu item names may vary between ghosts (for details, see the “[Right-click menu](rclick.md)” page).<br>
