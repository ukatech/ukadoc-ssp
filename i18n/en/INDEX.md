# Introduction

Welcome to SSP!

## What is SSP?

To put it grandly, SSP is a desktop agent system runtime; more plainly, it is a desktop mascot; and, simply put, it is “something nice to have around.”

It may not be especially useful for anything in particular, but it might add a little charm to your machine.

## What can I do with it?

SSP is highly compatible with a wide variety of character data (“ghosts”) for Ukagaka, with over a thousand available. You can easily and conveniently display lots of characters on your desktop.

Its lightweight operation won’t affect your usual work or get in your way.

…Though if you get caught up with your ghost, that’s another matter.
